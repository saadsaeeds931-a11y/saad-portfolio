FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know before we start working together.

How long does a website take to complete?+
A standard business website typically takes 7–14 days. eCommerce stores and larger projects may take 3–4 weeks. I'll give you a clear timeline estimate before we start.
Do you redesign existing WordPress websites?+
Absolutely. I specialize in WordPress redesigns — modernizing outdated sites while preserving your existing SEO authority and content.
Can you build a WooCommerce online store?+
Yes! WooCommerce development is one of my core specialties. I set up product listings, payment gateways (Stripe, PayPal), shipping configurations, and custom checkout flows.
Do you provide website maintenance after launch?+
Yes. I offer ongoing maintenance packages that include plugin/theme updates, security monitoring, backups, and content updates so your site stays secure and up-to-date.
Can you improve my existing website's speed?+
Definitely. I offer standalone speed optimization services including image compression, caching setup, database optimization, CDN integration, and Core Web Vitals improvements.
Do you build websites for home service companies?+
Yes — this is my sweet spot! I've built websites for HVAC, plumbing, electrical, roofing, and other home service businesses. These sites are optimized for local SEO and phone call conversions.
